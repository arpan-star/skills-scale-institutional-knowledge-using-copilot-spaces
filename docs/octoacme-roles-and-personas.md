# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance / QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality strategy and acceptance standards for the project. They work closely with Product Managers, Developers, and Project Managers to ensure features meet agreed quality criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate quality standards, test strategy, and release readiness criteria
- Develop and maintain acceptance tests, regression tests, and QA checklists
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts with the delivery team
- Identify, document, and prioritize defects in partnership with engineering
- Validate that acceptance criteria are met before work is considered done
- Help refine Definition of Done and quality metrics for the team

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features with minimal defects and rework
- Improve confidence in release readiness and product quality
- Reduce cycle time by enabling earlier testing and clearer acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and sprint planning for acceptance testing
- Daily standups for QA progress, blockers, and dependencies
- Release-readiness reviews with PM, Product, and leadership
- Defect triage and prioritization with Developers and Technical Leads

### How this role interacts with others
- Works with Developers to validate implementation and triage bugs
- Collaborates with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and customer-facing quality expectations
- Supports Project Managers by providing visibility into readiness, risk, and release blockers

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own the technical direction and architecture for the solution. They help the team make sound design decisions, balance velocity with maintainability, and manage technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture, patterns, and integration approaches
- Lead design reviews and guide complex implementation decisions
- Identify technical debt, scalability concerns, and safety risks
- Mentor Developers and ensure code quality standards are consistently applied
- Partner with Product and Project leads on sequencing, dependencies, and scope trade-offs
- Support estimation and planning for technical complexity

### Goals
- Build systems that are maintainable, scalable, and secure
- Reduce technical risk and long-term maintenance cost
- Enable the team to deliver features without creating avoidable technical debt

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design workshops
- Code review and implementation guidance
- Risk discussions with PMs and Product Managers during planning
- Dependency and integration updates with other teams or stakeholders

### How this role interacts with others
- Works closely with Developers to guide implementation and resolve technical trade-offs
- Aligns with Product Managers on roadmap feasibility and delivery constraints
- Supports Project Managers by identifying dependencies, risks, and sequencing concerns

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Coaches help the team adopt effective agile practices, remove blockers, and improve delivery flow. They focus on team health, quality of execution, and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agile ceremonies such as standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers that slow the team down
- Coach the team on agile values, working agreements, and continuous improvement
- Maintain visibility into sprint progress and delivery health
- Help the team self-organize and improve predictability over time
- Track retrospective action items and follow up on improvement efforts

### Goals
- Enable a healthy, self-organizing, high-performing delivery team
- Improve predictability and delivery flow over time
- Create a culture of learning, transparency, and accountability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and improvement planning
- 1:1 coaching and team feedback sessions
- Cross-team coordination when process or dependency issues arise

### How this role interacts with others
- Supports Developers and Project Managers by improving team practices and reducing friction
- Helps Product Managers by ensuring backlog refinement and planning sessions stay focused and productive
- Partners with stakeholders and PMs to keep priorities, commitments, and risks visible

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors represent business interests and strategic priorities. They provide direction, approve scope and funding, and help ensure the project delivers value to the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, expected outcomes, and success metrics
- Approve project scope, budget, and timeline decisions
- Identify and engage key stakeholders and decision-makers
- Provide strategic guidance and resolve business-level trade-offs
- Escalate business-impacting risks or decisions that require executive support
- Champion the initiative and communicate value to broader groups

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Align delivery work with organizational priorities
- Maintain stakeholder confidence through clear communication and decisions

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based updates and steering reviews
- Go/no-go decision gates and approvals
- Escalation for risks, budget changes, or strategic pivots
- Release announcements and stakeholder-facing updates

### How this role interacts with others
- Works with Product Managers to confirm priorities and business outcomes
- Collaborates with Project Managers on milestones, dependencies, and escalations
- Provides business context and sponsorship to Developers and QA teams through clearly defined goals and trade-offs

---

## Security Officer / Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Officers and Security Champions ensure that products and processes meet required security and compliance standards. They help the team identify and mitigate security risks throughout the delivery lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements, standards, and review processes
- Conduct security reviews, threat assessments, and risk evaluations
- Coordinate vulnerability scanning, secure design review, and issue triage
- Review security-related architecture and design changes
- Support incident response and post-incident learning
- Help embed security awareness and best practices across the team

### Goals
- Reduce security risk and compliance issues before release
- Build secure products by default rather than as an afterthought
- Enable safe, fast delivery while preserving trust and resilience

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during planning and design discussions
- Requirements and acceptance checks for secure delivery
- Coordination with engineering during remediation and release sign-off
- Incident-related escalation and root cause review

### How this role interacts with others
- Works with Technical Leads and Developers to assess design risks and secure implementation choices
- Supports Product Managers and Project Managers with risk assessment and remediation planning
- Collaborates with QA to include security validation in release readiness and acceptance checks

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles are intentionally complementary: Product and Project leadership define outcomes and coordination, Developers and QA deliver and validate, Technical and Security roles reduce risk, and Sponsors and stakeholders provide business direction and accountability.
